# Portable arrival kernel

L0 procedural layer of this bundle: one domain-free kernel per clause, in
clause order. These carry no repository name, path, tool, or product. The
receipts that made each one survive contact live in the bundle's domain layer.

- K1 Answering "is this consumed?" requires enumerating every surface a
  consumer could arrive through, and reporting the surface set rather than a
  verdict. An audit that consults one surface has measured declaration, and
  declaration was never the question.
- K2 A capability a consumer *may* invoke is a planned island. Bind it to an
  exit the consumer already traverses and make the consumer's own receipt
  inadmissible without it; documentation is not a binding.
- K3 Record, per capability, the highest arrival an actual receipt supports,
  and treat every claim above that as a finding. Levels are raised by receipt
  readback alone - not by re-reading the same bytes, not by elapsed time.
- K4 A claim that a fact is recorded elsewhere is unverified until the
  elsewhere is opened and the exact value found. A false pointer to a record is
  worse than none: it retires suspicion without retiring risk.
- K5 A pointer into a tree you do not own is a standing claim that must be
  re-resolved on a cadence, and the owner's retirement of a target is a trigger
  to re-audit every row that names it. Unresolvable and unreadable are
  different states and must never be reported alike.
- K6 A finding terminates only in a durable home some process re-reads.
  Recommending a destination that does not exist, or a key nothing sweeps, is
  the finding failing to close. Where another owner already carries the closure
  law, point at it; a second copy drifts from the copy under test.
