# Portable supervision kernel (L0 procedural)

Domain-free kernels of the grounded clauses, one per clause. Receipts and the
self-defined machine primitives live in the domain layer; nothing here names a
concrete system.

- K1 A monitor reads; it never writes, and it speaks only at stage boundaries in receipt-quote + question form.
- K2 Count the decompression layers between the representation you checked and the property you claim; eliminate, carry semantics with, or negative-control every layer.
- K3 The first green is a review point; only terminal readback authorizes a consequential action.
- K4 Three same-shaped failures escalate to quarantine with named unblock conditions; reroute the fleet, never blind-repair.
- K5 Evidence regenerates through its producer; hand-edited evidence is laundering even when the edit is factually right.
- K6 Teardown mirrors construction and fires only after the justifying terminal state is read back; salvage to a durable ref before any reset.
- K7 Newly reachable invalid states are first-class deltas; sink each to the strongest enforcement layer that holds it, and let admission data catch up through its own change, never by widening the gate; when that change structurally cannot ride in the subject being judged, prove the unavailability, escalate to the gate owner with a receipt carrying the gate, its verbatim refusal and that reason, end unmerged, and accept an authorization only byte-pinned to the reviewed subject and retired by the landing it buys.
- K8 Bind every action to its exact subject identity, never to recency or position.
- K9 An oldest-first queue starves behind a head that keeps re-entering eligibility but can never terminate; park it honestly and cure the selection rule itself.
- K10 For dispatched background work, the completion notification is the only death certificate; silence, missing artifacts, and absence from process lists are all consistent with still-running.
- K11 A launcher's residual exit status describes one past run; health is the job's terminal log line plus direct artifact readback.
